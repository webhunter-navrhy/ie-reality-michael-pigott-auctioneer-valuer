Description

Michael Pigott Auctioneer & Valuer is delighted to present for sale this lovely 3 bedroom semi-detached property situated in a prime & mature residential area in Farranree.

The property occupies a corner plot which offers lots of space especially to the front with a spacious enclosed front garden with secure off street parking. To the rear, it has an enclosed raised decking area along with side access via a pedestrian gate. While, the house itself does require upgrading and modernization throughout, it offers tremendous potential for someone to acquire and put their own stamp and taste in becoming a perfect home in a super location in the northside of Cork City.

The property is most conveniently located just minutes to the City Centre and is within walking distance to all amenities and services available locally. It has easy access to regular public bus service and has easy access to the North Ring road network. It is also just minutes to Blackpool Shopping Centre, Blackpool Retail Park and is just minutes to Apple, one of Cork's major employers.

Viewing comes highly recommended.
